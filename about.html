What is Solitude to Solidarity?

Solitude to Solidarity (STS) is a student led non-profit group dedicated to raising awareness about loneliness. We raise awareness/funds and promote programs that foster social connections to combat loneliness in our communitites.

Our Mission

Our goals are simple, our aim is to:

  1. Spread public awareness throughout British Columbia about the health impacts of loneliness and lack of social connection.

  2. Raise funds for research and initiatives designed to reduce loneliness in the community.

  3. Initiate community-centered programs, initiatives, and activities to facilitate social relationships.
